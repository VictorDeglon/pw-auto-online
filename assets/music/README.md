# Music

- **The game's own songs** (`*.m4a`), made for Poway Automobile Online: *Descent into Luxury* (main theme),
  *Sunset Freeway* and *California Twilight* (exploring), *Out of Pocket* (electro), *California Dreamin'* and
  *Bad Investment* (radio). Wired up in `src/audio/music.js` and the KPOW station in `index.html`.
- **Kevin MacLeod** (`*.mp3`), incompetech.com, CC BY 4.0: the KMAC 102.3 and Lake Poway Lounge stations.
