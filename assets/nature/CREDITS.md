# Sketchfab plants

These trees and plants in `imp/` and `lod/` come from Sketchfab under **CC BY 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): credit the author, commercial use allowed. Each was changed for the
game by `tools/nature/bake_sf.sh`: centred, baked into an impostor atlas (`bake_impostor.py`, 8 x 8 views at 192 px)
and cut down to a near mesh (`fit_plant.py`: decimated, ambient occlusion in its vertex colours, textures at 256-512 px).
The PlantCatalog models are e-on software's PlantCatalog exports.
