# Sketchfab models

Every model in this folder comes from Sketchfab under the licence listed with it. Each one was changed for the game
by `tools/sketchfab/fit.py`: resized to real scale, simplified, its textures shrunk and re-encoded as WebP, its mesh
Draco-compressed; cars also had their wheels split out and their paint, glass and lamp materials renamed. The game
doesn't name the real makers; their names appear in these credits only.

CC BY-NC models are used non-commercially: the game is free, with no ads or paid items.
